Questions
Before you visit

    

      
What is an IMEI and why do you ask for it?
The IMEI is the 15-digit identity number of your phone. It helps us record exactly which phone is being repaired. Dial *#06# to see it, or check the phone box or Settings, About phone.

      
My phone is dead. How do I get the IMEI?
Tick the box on the repair form that says you cannot get the IMEI. You can also check the phone box, the SIM tray on some models, or the sticker under the battery on older phones. Otherwise we record it at the shop.

      
How long will my repair take?
It depends on the fault and the parts needed. We tell you what to expect after we have looked at your phone.

      
Can I order gadgets without coming to the shop?
Yes. Add items to your order and send it to us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability and the final price, then agree how you will pay and collect.

      
Do you sell new and used phones?
Our store shows the condition of each phone, new or UK used. Ask on WhatsApp for what is currently available.

      
Where are you located?
